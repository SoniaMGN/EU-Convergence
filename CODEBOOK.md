### Flags

| Flag | Meaning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| e | estimated | kept |
| p | provisional | kept |
| b | break in time series | kept |
| d | definition differs | kept |
| u | low reliability | dropped for employment and youth unemployment; kept for other series |
| : | not available | missing |

Missing means missing: no value was imputed, interpolated or edited. Levels are positive; rates lie within 0-100 (checked in validation).
